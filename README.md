# Clip Tool (test version)

Thanks for helping test Clip Tool! It's a Windows app for making YouTube videos: grab clips from YouTube links, record yourself and your screens, cut it all together in the Editor, and upload.

## Install

1. Open **Releases** on the right of this page and download **ClipToolSetup-…exe** from the newest one.
2. Run it. Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Type the testers' password you were given. You only do this once on each PC.
4. On Home, click **Set up Clip Tool** and follow the steps. You can skip any step and come back later.

## Updates

When a new version is out, Clip Tool tells you when it opens. Click **Update now** and it installs itself and reopens. You can also check any time in **Settings › General › Updates**.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11.
- For Chat and the AI tools: your own ChatGPT plan. The setup steps sign you in.
- For uploading to YouTube: the setup steps walk you through connecting your channel.

## Something wrong or confusing?

Click **? Help › Send feedback** (or **Settings › General › Send feedback**). Say what happened, and Clip Tool makes one zip file with your note, a picture of the app and its check-up. Your keys, sign-ins and videos are never in it. Send that file to whoever gave you Clip Tool.
